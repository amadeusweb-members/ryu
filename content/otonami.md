I'm a Japanese jazzy pop singer and songwriter, and I'd love to share my music with you. I write from a very intimate, expressive place, blending the warmth of jazz with my own original songwriting to create music that gently connects with the heart. My songs explore healing, hope, and the simple joy of being present in the moment.<!--more-->

In 2026, I released my self-produced album "Kimi no Waltz," featuring seven original songs plus a cover of "Amazing Grace." The sound is warm and acoustic-rich, with a light, airy rhythm underneath — bright, buoyant, and full of gentle melodic detail, built to lift the spirit rather than weigh it down. I continue performing both in Japan and internationally, building musical connections across borders, and I'm excited to keep sharing that feeling with new audiences.

Would love for you to give it a listen — I hope it brings a little warmth to your day.

Ryu Miho
