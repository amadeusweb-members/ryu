Jazzy Pop Singer, Composer, The Sigh of TOKYO. Born into a family with a trading company employee father, Ryu Miho spent early childhood overseas. After returning to Japan, Ryu Miho grew up in Kamakura, surrounded by music through a jazz-loving father and an R&B-loving mother. Serious musical activity began in high school, forming a rock band and taking up keyboard and guitar, as well as songwriting and composing, while collaborating with artists across a variety of genres.<!--more-->

In time, inspired by Helen Merrill, Ryu Miho became devoted to the world of jazz. Known as "The Sigh of TOKYO," Ryu Miho has drawn attention for a healing whisper voice, rich in overtones and a floating quality that experts have described as a "miraculous vocal cord." In 2009, the debut album "woman with LOVE" was released, revealing this talent in full bloom. In 2012, Ryu Miho made a major-label debut with the full album "...and you will find me" on King Records.

The response and reaction were so great that the second album "Because the Night…" was released little more than a year and a half later. A broad knowledge of music across genres, a cover repertoire that conveys a unique worldview infused with the rock spirit that serves as Ryu Miho's own spiritual backbone, high-quality original songs, intelligent beauty, richly individual vocal ability, and dazzling stage performance have together made Ryu Miho the talk of many circles as the emergence of a new DIVA. In 2014, Ryu Miho was appointed tourism ambassador for Nakanojo Town in Agatsuma District, Gunma Prefecture, singing the town's official theme song "Nakanojo no Uta," and continues to serve in that role.

In 2016, the 3rd album "Call me" was released on King Records. That same year, "Misty" won the Recording Newcomer Award. A fan of anime and games, Ryu Miho wrote the lyrics for and sang the theme of the 2019 animated film "COCOLORS," which was screened that year; wrote the lyrics for and sang "Light in you," the theme song for the 2020 TV commercial for the game Tales of Crestoria; and provided the AI voice for the 2022 console game "Yurukill."

In 2025, Ryu Miho released the LP album "Breeze of you" and sold out a first solo concert in Singapore. Ryu Miho also writes lyrics and composes music for other artists and commercials.
